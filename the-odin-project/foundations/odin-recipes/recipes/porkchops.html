Pork Chops

Description

Pork chops are a simple and delicious meal made with tender pork seasoned with herbs and spices. They are pan-seared until golden brown on the outside while remaining juicy and flavorful inside.

Ingredients

  • 4 bone-in center cut pork chops, 1-inch thick
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons kosher salt
  • 1 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 5 tablespoons butter, divided
  • 1 teaspoon fresh thyme
  • 8 ounces sliced mushrooms
  • 1 1/2 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce
  • Chopped parsley for garnish

Directions

  1. Season pork chops with salt, pepper, and garlic powder. Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add pork chops and cook until well browned. Flip and cook until done, then set aside.
  2. Melt 2 tablespoons of butter in the same skillet. Add garlic and thyme, then cook until fragrant. Add mushrooms and cook until well browned.
  3. Add Worcestershire sauce and water. Scrape the browned bits from the bottom of the pan and let the sauce simmer.
  4. Return the pork chops to the pan and coat them with the sauce. Serve topped with the pan drippings and chopped parsley.

Nutrition Facts (per serving)

  • 270 calories
  • 23g Fat
  • 6g Carbs
  • 12g Protein
