Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made with layers of pasta, rich tomato sauce, seasoned meat, creamy cheese, and melted mozzarella. It is baked until the cheese is golden and bubbly, making it a warm, hearty, and delicious meal.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ground beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Onion
  • Italian seasoning
  • Olive oil
  • Garlic
  • Black pepper

Steps

  1. Cook the lasagna noodles until tender.
  2. Brown the beef with onion and garlic, then add tomato sauce and seasoning.
  3. Layer the noodles, meat sauce, and cheeses in a baking dish.
  4. Bake until the cheese is melted and golden brown.
